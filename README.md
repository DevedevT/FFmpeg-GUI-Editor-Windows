# FFmpeg-GUI-Editor-Windows — Ready-to-Use Free Portable Video Editor (.exe)

**FFmpeg-GUI-Editor-Windows** is a lightweight, fast, and **free open-source video editor for Windows 10 and Windows 11** that works as an intuitive graphical user interface (**FFmpeg GUI**). Built specifically for users who want to **download a ready-to-use `.exe` executable** to trim, crop, compress, and convert videos without touching the command line.

No installation, no Python setup, no manual `PATH` configuration, and no watermarks. Just download the **pre-compiled `.exe`**, run it, and start editing. Licensed under **GPL v3**.

### 🎬 Core Video Editing Features
* **Lossless Video Trimmer & Cutter:** Cut and trim video start/end points with frame-level precision without re-encoding.
* **Smart Video Crop Tool:** Manual crop selection or automatic black-bar removal (**auto-crop**).
* **Video Compressor & Resizer:** Downscale 4K, 1440p, 1080p, or 720p videos to shrink file sizes for Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, and web upload.
* **Universal Video & Audio Converter:** Convert media files between **MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, MOV, GIF, APNG, MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG**, and more.
* **Rotate & Flip Video:** Fix vertical or upside-down videos in a single click.
* **Audio Volume Booster & Remover:** Increase/decrease volume or strip audio tracks from video files completely.
* **Duplicate Frame Remover:** Automatically detect and delete frozen or duplicate frames to reduce output file size.
* **Playback Speed & FPS Changer:** Speed up, slow down, or change video frame rate (FPS) effortlessly.
* **Chroma Key (Green Screen Removal):** Remove solid background colors from videos and images.

### ⚙️ Advanced Automation & FFmpeg Tools
* **Duplicate Scene Finder:** Scan and remove repeated video segments across multiple files.
* **Image Sequence to Video / GIF:** Turn a folder of PNG or JPG images into an MP4 video or animated GIF.
* **Built-in Subtitle Editor:** Create, edit, and sync `.srt` subtitles visually on the timeline.
* **Visual FFmpeg Command Generator:** Configure settings via GUI and copy the exact FFmpeg CLI arguments for your own scripts.
* **Batch Video Processing (`.bat`):** Export batch scripts to process dozens of videos via simple drag-and-drop.
* **Frame Extractor & Alpha Blend:** Save individual video frames as images or blend multiple frames together.
